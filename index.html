02 About

From dynamics model to a robot that moves

I gravitate toward the messy middle of robotics — where the dynamics model meets the physical robot. My coursework covers control systems, signals, and mechatronics, but I learn the most when a controller has to survive contact with the real world.

A lot of my work is bioinspired: swimming, flying, and folding robots that borrow their locomotion from biology, alongside optimal-control and autonomy projects on scale vehicles. I'm looking for an internship or new-grad role in robotics, controls, or autonomy where I can own a system from model to hardware bring-up.
